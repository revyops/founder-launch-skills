# Long chats and tokens

## What a token is

Tokens are what AI work is measured and paid in. Roughly, a token is a short word or part of a word. Every time you send a message, Claude re-reads the whole conversation so far, and all of that reading costs tokens.

So a conversation gets slower and more expensive the longer it runs. There is also a limit to how much it can hold at once (the context window). Near that limit, Claude summarises the older part to make room (compacting), and details from early in the chat can get lost.

Your plan includes an allowance. Heavy users pay for a higher plan or for extra usage. Efficient habits are worth building now.

## Habits that save tokens and get better answers

- **One chat per piece of work.** Do not run your support project and your ad campaign in the same conversation.
- **Hand off when the job changes**, not just because a chat is big. When a chat keeps losing detail after compacting, or the work moves to something unrelated, save and start fresh:

> "Make sure everything from this session is saved in the vault. Should I close this chat and start a new one? What is the most efficient way to keep going?"

  Then open a new chat and say "read the vault notes on X and carry on." A fresh chat that reads a good note beats a three-week-old chat.
- **Let the vault carry the memory**, not the conversation.
- **Point it at the notes it needs**, not the whole vault. "Read the support notes" is cheaper and clearer than "read everything".
- **Use a lighter model for simple side jobs** when the tool lets you choose, and keep the strongest model for work that needs judgement.

Claude will sometimes suggest a fresh start itself, but not always. Watching this is your job.
