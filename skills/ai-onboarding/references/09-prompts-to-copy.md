# Prompts to copy

Say these by voice and change the words to fit. Nothing here needs to be exact.

## Getting started

> "I'm new to working with AI. Walk me through getting set up one step at a time. Ask me what you need to know about my role and goals first, and wait for me to finish each step before moving on."

> "Help me lay out my notes vault. Here is what I do and the areas I work across: ... Propose a folder structure, explain it, and wait for my OK before creating anything."

## Loading context

> "Connect to [tool]. Then read everything from the last [six months] and write a summary into the vault of where things stand, what has been tried and what is unresolved."

> "Use the meeting recorder to find my calls about [topic] from the last [four months]. The titles may not say so, so check the transcripts. Pull out the key decisions and lessons and save them to the vault."

> "Research what recognised experts say about [subject]. Cite every source. Save it in the vault as a baseline we can argue with."

## Planning

> "Here is everything on my mind about [problem]: ... What do you think the right path is? Give me options with the trade-offs."

> "Before you build anything, show me the plan and tell me what you need from me."

> "Audit what is true today about [area], with the real numbers. Then compare it with the baseline and rank where we can improve."

## Building and running

> "Build me a report on [subject]. Use tabs, a row of headline numbers at the top, and tables whose rows open into a trend chart."

> "Run this every [Monday at 2am]. Put it in draft, send it to me in a direct message, and post it to [channel] once I approve."

> "Keep a to-do list in the vault of everything you are waiting on me for, across all our work."

## Teaching it

> "Write a skill from this, so next time it's done the way we just landed on."

> "Add that to the skill so you never do it again."

> "Read these examples of my writing and build a set of rules so your drafts sound like me. Check every draft against them before showing me."

## Checking

> "Go back through what you just told me and check every claim against its source. List anything you are not sure of."

> "If you can't get the data, say so and ask me. Never estimate or fill in."

> "Create a daily job that checks every other job ran, flags anything that looks off, and gives me a short list of what needs me today."

## Wrapping up

> "Make sure everything we learned and decided in this session is saved in the vault and the right skills. Tell me what you saved and where."

> "This chat is getting long. Is everything saved? Should I start a new one?"

## Onboarding someone else

> "Here is a transcript of a session where a colleague showed me how they use Claude. I want to do the same for [my area]. Walk me through getting started step by step, give me a checklist, and save the key lessons as a skill."
